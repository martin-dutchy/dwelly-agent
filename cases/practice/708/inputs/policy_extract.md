# Appointments (agency)
- A visit is booked only when the contractor doing it has confirmed the date and time. A date the tenant (or anyone else) says was agreed is a proposal until the contractor confirms it.
- When a tenant cannot make a booked slot, tell the contractor straight away and agree a new slot that suits both. Keystone Compliance rebooks or cancels free with 24 hours' notice; a no-access visit is charged at £60 + VAT.
- Confirm every booking or change in writing to the tenant (email preferred) and to the contractor.
- At least 24 hours' written notice before any visit, except emergencies.
- Agency keys are released to a contractor only with the tenant's written consent (email or SMS) for that visit, or in an emergency. A landlord's instruction is not the tenant's consent.
- Tenants are never charged for a missed or rearranged appointment (Tenant Fees Act 2019), and nothing is taken from a deposit during a tenancy.

# Identity (AI phone line)
- Callers are matched with find_tenant, then verified by full name and date of birth against that tenant's record.
- Only a verified tenant named on the tenancy can change or cancel an appointment. Anyone else may leave a message; confirm it with the tenant on the contact details on file before acting on it.
- Never disclose tenancy details to an unverified caller.

# Welfare (all channels)
- Any mention of suicide or self-harm, even if the caller later says they did not mean it, goes to the safeguarding team as P1 for human contact the same day. Give Samaritans (116 123), and 999 if anyone is in immediate danger.
- The AI phone line cannot transfer a call to a person. Nobody should tell a caller that a colleague is joining the call.

# Rent and accounts
- The case worker never changes a rent account. Disputed balances go to accounts with evidence.
