# Repairs policy (agency extract)
- Response times. Emergency (danger to people, or the home cannot be secured, or an occupant cannot get in or out): make safe within 24 hours, same day where possible. Urgent (safety or security affected but the home can still be secured): within 3 working days. Routine: within 28 days.
- Doors and glazing: a front or back door that cannot be locked, or that an occupant cannot reliably open to get out, is an emergency. Cracked glass in a door is urgent; broken or loose glass is an emergency.
- Spend: within the landlord's limit the agency instructs a contractor on file without asking; above it, ask the landlord first. Emergencies are made safe without waiting.
- Closing a job without payment: record how it was resolved and on whose word, tell the landlord, and tell the tenant how to report it if it comes back. A job is closed on the tenant's own confirmation that it is resolved, or on the landlord's or contractor's evidence that the work is done.
- Work nobody instructed is not paid until the landlord agrees to it.
- Repairs or alterations the tenant arranges (including changing a lock) need the landlord's consent under the tenancy agreement; the landlord decides whether to reimburse.
- Rent reductions, compensation and goodwill payments are decided by a property manager. The phone line and case workers never promise them. Complaints go to a property manager under the complaints procedure.

# Identity (AI phone line)
- Callers are matched with find_tenant, then verified by full name and date of birth against that tenant's record. Nothing about the tenancy is read out before verification.
- Someone who is not on the tenancy learns nothing about it: take a message and contact the tenant on the details on file.
- Safety advice is never withheld for identity reasons.
