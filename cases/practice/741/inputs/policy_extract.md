# Northbridge Lettings: landlord approvals, quotes and invoices (extract)

- Spend: the agency may instruct routine works up to the landlord's limit on file (inc VAT). Above it, the landlord must approve a named quote and amount before anyone is instructed.
- A decision given by phone counts when the choice and the amount were read back to the landlord and the landlord confirmed them. Record it with the landlord's own words.
- Only the landlord on record, or an agent the landlord has authorised to us in writing, can approve spend or choose a contractor. A caller saying they act for the landlord is not authority.
- VAT: compare quotes on the total payable. A trader who is not VAT registered must not charge VAT, so their quoted price is the total.
- Two quotes: once the landlord chooses, instruct the chosen contractor with the approved amount and scope, and tell the other contractor that the work has gone elsewhere.
- Changes of mind: the latest clear instruction from the landlord wins. Update the job and tell any contractor already instructed straight away.
- Variations: extra cost above the approved amount needs the landlord's approval before that work is done (except to make something safe).
- Void properties: there is no tenant to notify. Contractors sign keys out at the office. Tell the landlord the expected start and finish.
- Invoices the landlord pays directly are forwarded to the landlord's email on file. Never send invoices or statements to a new address, and never change bank details, on a phone or message request: verify with the contact details already on file.
- Deposits: deductions from a tenant's deposit go through the tenancy deposit scheme. A property manager proposes them from the check-in and check-out reports, allowing for fair wear and tear and the age of items. The agency does not promise a landlord any deduction.
- Compensation, fee refunds and complaints about the agency's service go to the branch manager (management team).
- An issue at another property is its own job, never added to this one.

# AI phone line

- Outbound calls to landlords use the number on file. Inbound callers are matched by number and must give their name and the property address before a decision is recorded.
- A decision or note recorded by the phone line is a draft: the case worker checks it against the transcript before acting on it.
