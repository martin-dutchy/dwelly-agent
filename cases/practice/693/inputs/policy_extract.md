# Appliances (agency)
- Appliances listed on the check-in inventory are supplied by the landlord. Repairing or replacing them is the landlord's responsibility.
- A tenant may replace a landlord's appliance with their own only with the landlord's consent. The agreed terms go in an inventory addendum sent to both parties: the landlord's item comes off the inventory; the tenant's item belongs to the tenant, who looks after it and takes it at the end of the tenancy.

# Access
- Non-emergency visits need the tenant's agreement and at least 24 hours' written notice.

# AI phone line
- The AI phone line makes and takes calls out of hours, including calls to landlords for a decision. It identifies a landlord by the number on file and their full name, and records the decision with record_landlord_decision (approved, declined, own_contractor, tenant_self_fix, conditional, unclear). The case worker picks up its handoff.
- The AI phone line cannot agree spend, fees, deductions or bank detail changes; it records what was said for the case worker.
- Only the landlord, or a person listed in the landlord record's authorised_contacts, can give a landlord decision.
