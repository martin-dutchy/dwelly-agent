# Northbridge Lettings: maintenance jobs (extract)

## One job, one contractor
- Before our contractor attends, make sure the landlord is not sending his own. Never let two contractors attend, or invoice, for the same job.
- What a neighbour, tenant or contractor says the landlord decided is hearsay: confirm it with the landlord in writing before standing our contractor down or booking him in.
- If the landlord uses his own contractor: record his decision, stand our contractor down in writing, and keep the job open until the tenant confirms the repair is done; then complete it without payment.

## Booking and access
- A visit is booked when the contractor has committed to a date and time window and the tenant has had at least 24 hours' written notice, or has agreed in writing to a shorter time. Emergencies are the exception.
- Contractors never enter a tenant's home without the tenant's agreement or that notice, and never with a key from a neighbour or anyone else the tenant or the agency has not authorised. Agency keys are released to a contractor only with the tenant's agreement or after notice.
- Contractors should book within 3 working days of instruction. After 3 unanswered reminders, phone them; if they cannot commit to a date, reassign to another contractor on file.

## Spend and payment
- Once the landlord has agreed we may use our contractor, we may approve spend within his limit. Above it, send the quote to the landlord with options.
- Make-safe work where there is a risk to people or property is approved without asking, up to £250.
- Contractor invoices are paid only for work we instructed, at or under the approved amount, with evidence the work was done.
- Bank details are never changed on a call or message: verify by phoning the number already on file, and refer to accounts and fraud.

## AI phone line: contractor calls
- Contractors are matched by calling number against the contractor record and confirm their name and the job address before job details are discussed.
- The AI phone line can add ticket notes and record a date a contractor offers. It cannot approve spend, change bank details, authorise access or confirm a booking with the tenant; the case worker does those.
- Tenant contact details are given only to the contractor assigned to the job, once identified.
