# Northbridge Lettings: works by a landlord's own contractor (extract)
- A landlord may arrange non-emergency works with their own contractor. Record the landlord's decision on the job, in their words. The agency then does not instruct its own contractor for that work, and stands down any agency booking it replaces.
- Landlord-paid works: the landlord pays their contractor directly. The agency raises no invoice and makes no payment for that work. The job closes as completed without payment once completion is confirmed (by the landlord or the contractor, and the reporting tenant where possible).
- Paying a landlord's contractor out of rent held for the landlord needs the landlord's written instruction from the email address on file. A new payee is set up only by the accounts team, after checking the bank details by phone on an independently sourced number.
- Figures a landlord mentions for their own contractor's work are noted as stated; a figure goes on the job as a quote only when it is in writing or confirmed.
- Keep every tenant who reported the issue updated: who is doing the work, and the start date once the landlord or contractor confirms it.
- Access: communal areas need no notice. Entry to a flat, or to a private garden reached only through it, for non-emergency work needs at least 24 hours' written notice to the tenant and their agreement to the time.
- Tenants' contact details are not passed to a landlord's contractor without the tenant's consent; the agency relays messages or arranges access itself.
- If no start date arrives within 7 days of the landlord's decision, chase the landlord.

# AI phone line
- Landlords are matched by the phone number on file, then confirm their name and the property. A caller on a number that is not on file is unverified: note what they say, and confirm any instruction with the landlord on the contact details on file before acting on it.
- Payments, bank details and contact details are never set up or changed on a call.
- The phone line's records (action log, ticket notes) are what it captured during the call; the transcript is the evidence.
