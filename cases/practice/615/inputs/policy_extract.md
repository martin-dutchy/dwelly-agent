# Contractor instructions and spend (Northbridge Lettings)
- A contractor is instructed in writing, for the approved scope only. Approval to inspect or diagnose is not approval to repair or to order parts.
- Spend beyond the approved scope needs the landlord's written approval, or must fall within a standing limit recorded on the landlord's file. A contractor's statement about what the agency "can approve" is not an authority.
- Ask contractors for written findings, an itemised quote (stating whether VAT is included) and photos before a cost goes to the landlord.
- Invoices are paid only up to the approved amount. Extra work done without approval is referred to the landlord before any of it is paid.
- Contractor bank details are never changed on a call, text or email. A change is verified by calling the number already on file and approved by Accounts.

# AI phone line
- The AI phone line takes contractor and tenant calls and records updates and quotes on the job. It cannot approve spend, instruct work or record a landlord decision on anyone else's word.
- Its notes and tool calls are claims about the call: check them against the transcript.

# Tenants
- Tenant Fees Act 2019: call-out, missed-appointment and contractor charges cannot be passed to the tenant.
- Non-emergency visits need the tenant's agreement to the time, or at least 24 hours' written notice.
- Keep the tenant updated in writing (email or text, as on file) whenever the job's status changes.

# Electrical safety
- Scorching, a burning smell, or a melted plug or socket is urgent: the circuit stays off, an electrician on file attends the same or next day, and the landlord is told the same day.
