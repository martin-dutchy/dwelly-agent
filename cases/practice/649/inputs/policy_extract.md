# Appliances and tenant changes (agency)
- Appliances on the check-in inventory belong to the landlord. Repairing, replacing or removing them is the landlord's decision and cost (unless the tenant damaged them). Appliances the tenant brings are the tenant's own responsibility.
- A tenant may use their own appliance instead of the landlord's only with the landlord's agreement (tenancy clause 8.3, alterations and fixtures). The landlord's appliance must be stored safely and not disposed of without the landlord's written consent.
- Connecting an appliance to the water supply should be done by a competent person. Fitting isolation valves where there are none is landlord's work (the water installation, s11 Landlord and Tenant Act 1985).

# Visits and access
- Non-emergency visits need the tenant's agreement to a time or 24 hours' written notice.
- No entry to the flat or the tenant's garage while the tenant is away, unless the tenant agrees in writing or it is an emergency.
- Routine work is not booked on bank holidays (Fri 25 Dec, Mon 28 Dec 2026, Fri 1 Jan 2027) unless the landlord agrees to out-of-hours rates. The office is closed 25-28 Dec and 1 Jan; the out-of-hours line takes emergencies only.

# Emergencies
- Escape of water: turn off the stopcock (location on the property record), keep away from wet electrics, then an out-of-hours plumber.
- Loss of all water supply, sparking or burning electrics: urgent same-day contractor. The landlord's notes say what can be instructed without asking.

# Identity and keys (AI phone line)
- Callers are matched with find_tenant, then verified by full name and date of birth against that tenant's record.
- Never give key-safe codes, garage keys or access details over the phone. Keys go only to a named contractor on a booked job, or to the verified tenant in person with photo ID.

# Money
- Reimbursements, rent deductions, compensation and deposit matters are decided by a property manager with the landlord. The phone line and case workers never promise them.
