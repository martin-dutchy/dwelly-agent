Help test an AI phone assistant 📞

A 3-minute role-play call for a hackathon project: an AI assistant for a lettings agency, tested by real people on real phone lines.

  1. Enter your phone number.
  2. You get a character card: who you are and what to say.
  3. Press Call me now and the assistant rings you. Play your part in your own words.

UK numbers get a phone call. Leave it blank, or use a non-UK number, to talk from your browser instead.

Your number is used only to ring you when you press the button: at most 3 calls a day. Nothing is sold or shared.
