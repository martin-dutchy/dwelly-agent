You'll phone an AI assistant for a lettings agency and play a tenant with a problem. Each call takes 2 to 5 minutes. We're testing how well it copes with real people on real lines, so there's no right or wrong way to talk.

  • Your call is recorded. The audio and transcript are stored by ElevenLabs (the voice provider) and by us, and used only to improve this assistant.
  • Use the made-up details on the card. Don't give any real personal information.
  • Nothing you say will really happen. No repairs or messages are sent.
