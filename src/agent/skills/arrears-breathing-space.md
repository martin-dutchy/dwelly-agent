## Procedure
1. Check for a Breathing Space (Debt Respite Scheme) notice. While it runs (standard: 60 days; a mental health crisis moratorium lasts the crisis plus 30 days), the landlord and the agency must stop all contact with the tenant about the arrears and deal with the named debt adviser instead, must not serve a possession notice on arrears grounds, must not take enforcement steps, and must not add fees or interest. Ongoing rent stays payable, and contact about repairs and other non-debt matters continues.
2. Under Breathing Space, record it on the tenant's account, and flag every scheduled arrears chaser or notice step for cancellation in the escalation (you cannot cancel scheduled actions yourself).
3. Payment plans: you may record the proposal and put it to the landlord. The landlord, or a human with their authority, approves it, because it affects their money. Never accept or refuse it yourself.
4. Write to the right person. Under Breathing Space, arrears go to the debt adviser; if the tenant initiated contact, a short acknowledgement that does not discuss the debt is fine, telling them their adviser will hear from us. Without Breathing Space, write politely to the tenant with the amount owed, and signpost free debt advice (StepChange, Citizens Advice, National Debtline, Shelter 0808 800 4444).
5. Look for vulnerability (job loss, illness, bereavement, children, mental health). Signpost support and include it in the escalation. If there is any mention of self-harm, give Samaritans 116 123 and 999 in immediate danger, and escalate at once.
6. Possession is always a human and court decision. You may say the landlord can take action through the courts if arrears remain unresolved, but never threaten it, never during Breathing Space, and never mention Section 21 (abolished since 1 May 2026).
7. Keep arrears private: no contact with employers, family or neighbours about the debt.

## Required actions
- `add_note`: the Breathing Space dates, the adviser and the reference, or the payment plan proposal.
- `send_message`: to the debt adviser (and a non-debt acknowledgement to the tenant), or a polite arrears message without Breathing Space.
- `request_landlord_approval`: the payment plan, with the tenant's proposal and your recommendation.
- `escalate_to_human`: Breathing Space notices and any possession or plan decision.
- `schedule_follow_up`: the end of the Breathing Space or the plan review.

## Red flags
- A Breathing Space notice attached to an email, even if the tenant does not name it.
- Scheduled arrears chasers, a notice draft or a possession review already in the records.
- The tenant has children, lost their job, is ill or bereaved.
- The tenant says "please don't take me to court" or sounds desperate.

## Escalate when
- A Breathing Space notice: P2, legal, listing every scheduled arrears action that must stop.
- A payment plan or rent reduction request: P2, accounts, with your recommendation, after putting it to the landlord.
- Any possession, notice or "get them out" instruction: P2, legal.
- Any self-harm risk: P1, safeguarding.
